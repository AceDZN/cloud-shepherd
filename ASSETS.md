## Settings and processing

Generation settings for both new models:

- Text-to-3D, HD Model, AI model **H3.1 – Best Quality**
- Texture on, 2K, PBR on, Remove Lighting on
- Triangle topology, polycount 20000
- Privacy: Private
- T-Pose off

Exports: GLB, 2k textures. For the farmer, "Export Skeleton" and "Animation stay in Place" were both on.

Raw downloads are kept in `assets-src/tripo/<name>_raw.glb`. Optimized files are in `public/models/<name>.glb`, all made with this command (@gltf-transform/cli 4.5.1):

```
npx -y @gltf-transform/cli optimize assets-src/tripo/<name>_raw.glb public/models/<name>.glb \
  --compress false --simplify false --texture-compress webp --texture-size 1024
```

- No meshopt, Draco or quantization, so plain `GLTFLoader` loads the files with no decoder. This also avoids the skinned-bounds bug seen in Slope Sled.
- Textures are 1024² WebP, which requires `EXT_texture_webp` (three.js supports it natively).
- Static props also carry `KHR_materials_volume` from Tripo, which is harmless.
- Each optimized file was checked with `npx -y @gltf-transform/cli inspect`.

**Conventions (all models):**

- Y-up, base at y = 0, largest dimension ≈ 1 unit (Tripo normalizes). Scale in code.
- Each file is one mesh with one material.
- Tripo's front-view convention is **+Z = front**.
- Front axes below were checked from geometry/texture (eye/headlight/toe positions), not assumed.

Initial-load total of all 9 optimized files: **7,300,684 bytes (≈7.0 MB)**.

## Per model

### anani.glb — ענני, the hero cloud (generated)

- **Task:** https://studio.tripo3d.ai/workspace/generate/ca70b4c4-a358-4e83-9968-20b8d977de28
- **Prompt:** "Stylized cartoon game character: a cute round fluffy white cloud spirit mascot, a plump cumulus cloud body made of soft rounded puffy lobes, slightly wider than tall, with a simple happy face on the front: two big glossy black oval eyes with white highlights, small rosy cheeks and a small open grin. No arms, no legs, no props, no ground, no base. Soft pure white and very light blue-grey colors, smooth toy-like matte material, Pixar style, chunky simple readable silhouette, clean low-to-mid poly mobile game style, single isolated character, front view."
- **Ownership:** generated by this crew in the user's account (private).
- **Cost:** 30 displayed. Balance 2970 → 2910; the 60 drop includes another crew's job.
- **Look:**
  - A round, lumpy white cumulus with big glossy black eyes, pink cheeks and an open pink grin.
  - Despite "no arms, no legs", Tripo added two small puff "nub" arms at the front sides and two little puff feet underneath. These read as part of the cloud.
- **Rig:** none. Not rigged on purpose:
  - Tripo's auto-rig is Humanoid or "Other" (quadruped), and "Other" only offers a walk preset (see the Slope Sled dog).
  - Neither gives squash/stretch or a blink. The eyes and grin are baked into the texture, so a rig can't blink them.
  - Do squash/stretch, bob and growth with object scale.
  - Darken toward slate by lerping `material.color` (it multiplies the white baseColor map; the eyes stay dark).
  - For a blink, scale a small procedural white eyelid quad over the eyes, or briefly squash y.
- **Front:** face toward **+Z**. Measured: dark eye/mouth texels have centroid (0.03, 0.66, +0.29).
- **Pivot:** the bottom sits at y = 0. Offset by −0.5 in y to rotate and scale around the centre.
